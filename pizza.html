CHICKEN PIZZA

Ingridient

  • Pizza dough
  • Pizza sause
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Cooked chicken
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Tomato
  • Oregano
  • Chili flakes

Recipe

  1. Spread the pizza sause over the dough
  2. Sprinkle a layer of mozzarella cheese and the cooked chicken evenly and onion capsicum and origano and chili flakes
  3. Bake for 12-15 min until the cheese melts and the crust turns golden
  4. Remove from the oven,let it cool for 2-3 min than slice and serve hot
